About Solar Sphere

Solar Sphere is an educational website designed to make space exploration interesting and easy to understand. It introduces visitors to the planets, the Sun and the fascinating world beyond Earth.


Our goal is to encourage curiosity about astronomy and inspire everyone to learn more about the universe.
